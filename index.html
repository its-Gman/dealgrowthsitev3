Common Questions

What You're
Probably Wondering

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients see pipeline movement within two to four weeks, once the AI has mapped your market and the first campaigns launch. Revenue impact typically lands at the 60 to 90 day mark, depending on your sales cycle.

Is this compliant for financial institutions?

Yes. All outreach runs through domains and infrastructure configured for deliverability and auditability. We log every send, every reply, and every opt-out. If your compliance team needs custom data retention or approval workflows, we build those in during integration.

How is this different from hiring SDRs?

SDRs take 90 days to ramp, cost $60K to $100K each, and churn. Our systems go live in weeks, run 24/7, and get smarter with every campaign. You get the output of a full outbound team for less than the cost of a single hire.

Will this work with our existing stack?

We integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack, Gmail, Outlook, and most major CRMs and communication tools. Your team keeps working the way they always have. Their lead flow is the only thing that changes.

What does the investment look like?

Engagements scale from single-workflow builds to full GTM architecture, typically in the $5K to $10K monthly range. We scope precisely during the strategy call and only propose what delivers a clear, measurable return. No long-term lock-in required.
